Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the online store collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by customers when placing orders, making payments, requesting cancellations, or requesting returns or exchanges.

1. Introduction

The store currently operates using guest checkout. Customers do not need to create a user account or maintain a customer profile to place an order.

2. Information We Collect

When a customer places an order, the store may collect the information required to process and deliver that order, including full name, email address, phone number, delivery address, city, state, country, postal code, and optional order notes.

Order records also contain information about the purchased products and variants, including product name, SKU, size, color, quantity, original price, selling price, discount percentage, total price, and savings.

For online payments, payment-related records may include a Razorpay order ID and payment ID. Payment processing is handled through the payment provider rather than by storing customers’ card or banking credentials in the store’s order model.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to process and fulfill orders, communicate with customers, validate delivery availability by pincode, process online payments, manage cancellations, handle returns and exchanges, generate invoices, and provide order-related support.

The store may use the customer’s order number and email address to allow a guest customer to look up an order and access order-related cancellation or return functionality.

4. Payment Information

Online payments are processed through Razorpay. The store records payment status and transaction identifiers necessary to associate a payment with an order. Customers should review Razorpay’s applicable privacy and payment terms for information about data processed directly by the payment provider.

5. Order, Cancellation and Return Information

Order information is retained as part of the store’s order records. Cancellation requests may include the customer’s stated cancellation reason. Return or exchange requests may include the selected products, quantities, request type, reason, administrative notes, and exchange-related product and price information.

6. Invoice and Cloud Storage

Generated invoice files may be stored using Cloudinary storage. Invoice paths are associated with the relevant order number. Access to stored files should be restricted through the application’s operational and hosting controls.

7. Data Protection

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

The store may use third-party services required for payment processing and media or file storage, including Razorpay and Cloudinary. Those services may process information according to their own policies and terms.

9. Customer Rights and Requests

Customers may contact the store regarding their order information, correction of inaccurate information, or other privacy-related questions. Requests may be subject to verification to protect customer and order information.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the store’s services, technology, or legal requirements change. The latest version published on the website will apply to information handled under the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, customers should contact the store using the official contact details published on the website.
